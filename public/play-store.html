App permissions

Required only for AI food photo logging. Photos are processed by OpenAI and not stored permanently on our servers.
Notifications
Push reminders for hydration, meals, sleep, and workout goals. Fully opt-in and configurable in Settings.
Health Data
Access to device activity data (optional) to auto-populate step counts and workout sessions.
Internet
Required to sync your health logs, load AI insights, and connect to FitPulseBot servers.
Requesting data deletion
In-app deletion: Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Account. Deletion is permanent and completes within 30 days.
By email: Send a request to privacy@fitpulsebot.fit with subject "Delete My Account". We'll confirm within 7 business days.
Data export: Download all your health data in JSON format before deletion from Settings → Data & Privacy → Export.
Partial deletion: You can delete individual health log entries (meals, workouts, sleep entries) at any time without deleting your account.
