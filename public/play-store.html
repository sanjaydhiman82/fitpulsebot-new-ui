Requesting data deletion
In-app: Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Account. Permanent, completes within 30 days.
By email: privacy@fitpulsebot.fit with subject "Delete My Account". Confirmed within 7 business days.
Data export: Download all health data in JSON from Settings → Data & Privacy → Export before deletion.
